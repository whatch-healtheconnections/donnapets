Meet Donna

Hello neighbors! I grew up with animals my whole life and have a passion for pets of all sizes and species. I founded Donna's Personal Pet Care to ensure your babies have a reliable, loving sitter while you are away.

I am Pet CPR Certified and my sister is a Licensed Vet Tech who is just a phone call away, bringing a high level of medical awareness to every visit.

My mission is to create a stress-free environment where your pets don't just survive—they thrive with personalized attention and affection.
